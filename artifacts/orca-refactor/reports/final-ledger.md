# Final refactor ledger — 100 principles

Consolidated from the nine original batch reports, using their recorded evidence rather than inventing missing checks. Each numbered row has an explicit disposition. **已套用** = at least one batch documents application (or compliance); **不適用／保留** = evidence says not applicable or intentionally kept; **未記錄** = the original report does not explicitly record this item. A batch’s absence of an entry is not a claim it performed the work. Skillshare has a progress log rather than a numbered ledger, so only documented step evidence is mapped. Conflicts follow [principles.md](../tools/principles.md): YAGNI over patterns without real variants; no Yoda; no unrelated formatter pass. Existing unchanged behavior may be intentionally retained. Original report evidence is reproduced with source names.
